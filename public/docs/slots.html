Slots & Shortcuts

The pattern pad gives you ten one-key shortcuts to the patterns you use most, so you can jump between looks without touching the mouse.

Assigning a pattern to a slot

Found a pattern you like? Drag it from the library into one of the ten pattern pad slots, labeled 1 through 0. Your layout is saved and restored the next time you open the app.

Keyboard shortcuts

With the control window focused, press a number key to play that slot's pattern instantly:

KeyAction
1–9, 0Play the pattern in that slot
Two number keys togetherBlend two patterns (see Blending)
Shift + numberStage that pattern as a CUE (see Cue Mode)
EnterTake a cued pattern live
EscCancel a cue

Tip Reordering the pad is just a drag-and-drop — the keyboard shortcuts always follow the current layout.
