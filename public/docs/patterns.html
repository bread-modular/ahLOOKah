Patterns & Parameters

A pattern is one audio-reactive visual. There are 58 of them, organized into themed groups. Click one and it appears on screen instantly.

Browsing the library

The pattern library sits in the middle of the control panel. Patterns are grouped by theme:

  • Rhythmic — beat-driven shapes like Circles, Bars, and Pulse Rings.
  • 3D — rotating, depth-mapped structures.
  • Cinematic / Shaders — GPU shaders and atmospheric looks.
  • Neon / Lasers — glow lines and light effects.
  • Video FX — camera-input effects like chroma key and kaleidoscope.
  • Glitch / Effects — RGB split, scanlines, CRT, and distortion.
  • Basics — solid color, gradient wash, checkerboard, and more.

Tweaking parameters

Every pattern exposes its own sliders. The Parameters panel on the right updates to match the selected effect — bass/mid/high responsiveness, counts, speeds, and more. Drag a slider and the change applies to the output live, so you can shape a look while it's on the projector.

Tip Most patterns react to three frequency bands — bass, mid, and high. The responsiveness sliders control how strongly each band drives the visual.
