Patterns & Parameters

A pattern is one visual look. The library is organized into themed groups, with audio-reactive effects and a deliberately non-reactive checkerboard. Click one and it appears on screen instantly.

Browsing the library

The pattern library sits in the middle of the control panel. Patterns are grouped by theme:

  • Simple — first in the library: Circles, Bars, Dot Grid, Pulse Stripes, Cross Pulse, Diamond Tiles, Radial Spokes, and Checkerboard.
  • Rhythmic — beat-driven shapes like Pulse Rings, Beat Weave, and Ripple Lattice.
  • 3D — rotating, depth-mapped structures, including Polygon Tunnel and Orbital Cages.
  • Cinematic / Shaders — GPU shaders and atmospheric looks, including lightweight Silk Flow and Prism Caustics.
  • Neon / Lasers — glow lines and light effects, including Laser Fan and Neon Hex.
  • Video FX — camera-input effects like chroma key, kaleidoscope, edge glow, thermal color, prism split, ripple lens, and mirror tiles.
  • Glitch / Effects — RGB split, scanlines, CRT, Data Rain, and Signal Tear.
  • Basics — solid color, color wash, color bars, noise static, and film grain.

Fast building blocks

The five new Simple shapes use bounded, single-density Canvas2D drawing. The new shader and camera effects use single passes instead of heavy raymarching. Each has bass, mid, and high responsiveness controls; set a band to zero to remove its contribution. Hover a library button to see what each band changes.

Checkerboard is not audio reactive. It starts as stationary black-and-white squares. Change cell size, hues, saturation, lightness, or optional manual drift. Neither audio nor old saved audio-pulse settings can change its size or speed. It stays in Simple and is the default source when you add a mapping inside a Projection Mapping pattern; existing mappings keep their sources.

Camera effects require permission on the output window; the control preview is a placeholder. The new effects include a mirror switch and share one camera stream across LIVE and CUE. Thermal is a false-color visual effect, not a temperature sensor.

Tweaking parameters

Every pattern exposes its own sliders. The Parameters panel on the right updates to match the selected effect — bass/mid/high responsiveness, counts, speeds, and more. Drag a slider and the change applies to the output live, so you can shape a look while it's on the projector.

Tip Most patterns react to three frequency bands — bass, mid, and high. The responsiveness sliders control how strongly each band drives the visual.
